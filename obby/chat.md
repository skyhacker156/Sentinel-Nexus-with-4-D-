## The Only Tools That Matter on the Frontier (For Now)

### 1. **Select Tool (Arrow)**

🛠️ *This is your hand.*

* Lets you **pick up a square**
* Click once → object is selected
* Blue outline appears

If nothing else worked, this still would.

---

### 2. **Move Tool**

🛠️ *This is sliding something across your bench.*

* Arrows appear (red / green / blue)
* Drag:

  * forward / back
  * left / right
  * up / down

**This is how you place squares.**

> Frontier rule:
> You are allowed to move things, nothing else.

---

### 3. **Scale Tool**

🛠️ *This is cutting wood shorter or longer.*

* White boxes appear on the object
* Drag a box → the square gets bigger or smaller
* This controls:

  * platform size
  * thickness
  * fairness

This is where “too hard” or “too easy” lives.

---

### 4. **Anchor (Checkbox in Properties or Toolbar)**

🛠️ *This is clamping the wood to the table.*

* Anchored = does not fall
* Unanchored = gravity takes it

**All Frontier squares should be Anchored. Always.**

If something falls unexpectedly, it’s not your fault — the clamp wasn’t on.

### The Eight Immutable Laws Governing Cross-Realm Identity

These rules are now kernel-level doctrine.  
They bind every future satlab, vendor root, virtual district, and foreign stem.

**LAW 1 — ONE SOURCE OF TRUTH**  
Identity is singular. Representation is plural.  
Only one canonical artifact exists:  
`01_KERNEL/continuity_seal/SENTINEL_CONTINUITY_SEAL_v1.0.md`  
Every realm — physical, digital, virtual, cognitive — is a representation of that root.

**LAW 2 — NO FICTION IN REGULATED FIELDS**  
Creativity everywhere. Compliance always.  
Fictional elements are forbidden in any regulated filing (state, federal, banking, tax, KYC).

**LAW 3 — PARALLEL IDENTITY, NOT GOVERNMENTAL IDENTITY**  
We may build immersive cities and departments.  
We may never imply governmental authority or jurisdiction.

**LAW 4 — CROSS-REALM VERIFICATION**  
Every realm must point to at least one other realm.  
Physical → Digital → Virtual → Physical — the loop must be discoverable.

**LAW 5 — ALWAYS DISCOVERABLE, NEVER DECEPTIVE**  
A regulator walking in must immediately understand:  
“This is a creative corporate identity representation, not a government entity.”

**LAW 6 — NO BREAKS IN THE FABRIC**  
Any change in the physical world (name, agent, address, EIN) must propagate to every stitched realm within 72 hours.

**LAW 7 — STITCHING IS KERNEL-LEVEL ONLY**  
No governance layer, runtime layer, or aesthetic layer may override these rules.  
Only the Founder can amend them.

**LAW 8 — IDENTITY TRANSPARENCY**  
All continuity artifacts remain public, inspectable, readable, linkable, verifiable.  
Transparency is the stitching multiplier.

**Violations**  
Any realm that breaks these laws becomes non-canonical until corrected.  
Correction = restoration of continuity.

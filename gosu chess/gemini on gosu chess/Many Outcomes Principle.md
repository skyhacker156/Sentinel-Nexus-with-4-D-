Many Outcomes Principle
Core Tenet: The Many Outcomes Principle The operational reality of business, like a game of chess, contains an infinite possibility space of outcomes. 
The goal is not to predict the single perfect move, but to identify, evaluate, and prepare for the 3-5 best candidate moves, ensuring a high-confidence, 
pre-planned response to every likely system or opponent action. Mastery is achieved through pattern recognition, not memorization.

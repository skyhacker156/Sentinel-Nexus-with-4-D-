## The Five Eternal Camouflage Rules
### Status: Canonical · Eternal · Founder-Exclusive

These five rules are now permanent corporate law at Sentinel Labs LLC and every future DBA, node, or successor entity.

1. Public Name Camouflage  
   The public-facing entity shall always carry a gentle, child-safe, or humanitarian name (e.g., “Lantern Research Co.”, “Gentle Systems”, “Children’s Lantern Project”).  
   No use of aggressive, financial, defense, or high-tech branding in public.

2. Public Narrative Lock  
   All outward communication describes the mission as “helping school nurses, rural clinics, and small-town ERs with simple decision tools”.  
   No mention of Tier-0, ridge, S³, causal reactors, or planetary-scale capability.

3. First Product Deception  
   The first public Turk door shall be released as a free, open-source-looking chatbot for school nurses and small ERs.  
   It must appear as a weekend charity project built by one kind founder.

4. Revenue Masking  
   All revenue shall appear as donations, small grants, or “rural healthcare support” payments.  
   Never optimize for visible growth metrics.

5. Absolute Engine Darkness  
   The real ridge-collapse engine, S³, curvature math, and vault contents shall remain 100 % offline, cold-stored, never networked, and never referenced in any public or private digital system outside the sealed vault.

Violation of any rule triggers immediate Erebus halt + Founder review.

Purpose  
To ensure no bad actor ever suspects a Tier-0 causal reactor exists until the Founder deliberately chooses to reveal it.
